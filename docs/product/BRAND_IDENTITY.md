# KINNIPAW — Provisional Brand Identity

## Brand status

**KINNIPAW (K-I-N-N-I-P-A-W) is the selected working name, not a cleared trademark.** An initial exact-name web search did not surface an obvious same-category animal-care platform. This does not establish domain availability, app-store availability, company-name availability or legal clearance. A generic pet-name article contains the word as a suggested pet name, so distinctiveness must be professionally reviewed.

Before public launch, check:
- Bangladesh DPDT trademark records and relevant service/goods classes.
- WIPO Global Brand Database and major target-market registers.
- Domain registration/WHOIS or registrar availability for the preferred domains.
- Google Play and Apple App Store names and confusingly similar names.
- Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and other social handles.
- GitHub organization/repository and package registry namespace.
- Pronunciation, spelling, unintended meanings and transliteration in Bangla and English.

Do not buy a domain, commission final brand assets or promise exclusivity until this review is complete.

## Working identity

- **Name:** KINNIPAW (K-I-N-N-I-P-A-W)
- **Tagline:** One Platform. Every Animal. One Connected Ecosystem.
- **Category:** Pet community, responsible adoption, lost-and-found, rescue coordination and animal-care platform.
- **Initial market:** Bangladesh.
- **Languages:** Bangla and English.
- **Brand character:** Warm, trustworthy, calm, modern, helpful, inclusive and responsible.

## Positioning statement

KINNIPAW brings animal guardians, animal lovers, responsible adopters, rescuers, organizations and veterinary providers into a safer, more useful community where people can care for animals, find responsible adoption opportunities, report lost or found animals and coordinate help.

## Brand promise

Make everyday pet care more organized and make it easier for communities to help animals when it matters.

## Differentiation

The product must not be presented as merely another photo-sharing feed or pet shop. Its differentiated value is the combination of:
1. Pet-centered profiles and memories.
2. Responsible adoption workflows.
3. Lost-and-found discovery and reunification support.
4. Moderated rescue coordination.
5. Care records and reminders.
6. Local, trustworthy community and service discovery.
7. Bangla-English accessibility for Bangladesh.

## Tone of voice

- Friendly, clear and respectful.
- Never shame owners or adopters.
- Avoid false medical certainty or fear-based copy.
- Use plain Bangla and simple English.
- Be transparent about verification: distinguish verified, unverified and community-reported information.

## Visual direction

Warm editorial + modern consumer technology. Premium and emotionally engaging, but not childish or cartoon-heavy.

Suggested starting tokens (subject to contrast/accessibility testing):
- Warm ivory: `#FAF9F6`
- Sage green: `#66856C`
- Soft terracotta: `#D98769`
- Deep charcoal: `#252A27`
- Soft neutral: `#E8E6E0`
- White surface: `#FFFFFF`

Typography direction: Geist/Geist Sans for Latin text and Noto Sans Bengali (or a tested alternative) for Bangla. Confirm font licensing and rendering before implementation.

Avoid relying on a generic paw-print as the only brand mark. Explore an ownable symbol combining connection, care, and companion identity.

## Colour decisions

- **Primary:** Forest Green `#245B45` — decision per ADR-020.
- **Supporting / Secondary:** Sage Green `#66856C`.
- **Conflict C-4 resolved:** Original source was HTML Foundation (`#245B45` Forest Green); ZIP documentation had `#66856C` Sage Green as primary. The owner arbitrated that Forest Green `#245B45` is the primary brand colour, and Sage Green `#66856C` serves as the supporting/secondary colour.
- **Extended palette:**
  - Warm Ivory: `#FAF9F6` (background/paper)
  - Soft Terracotta: `#D98769` (accent)
  - Deep Charcoal: `#252A27` / `#17211D` (typography/ink)
  - Soft Neutral: `#E8E6E0` / `#F0F4EE` (panel surfaces)
  - White: `#FFFFFF` (surface cards)

Use lowercase `kinnipaw` for repository and technical identifiers. Suggested package scope: `@kinnipaw/*`. These are working conventions only; verify namespace availability before publication.
