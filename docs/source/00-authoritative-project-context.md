# KINNIPAW — Authoritative Project Context (Read First)

## Product identity
- Exact working name: **KINNIPAW** (K-I-N-N-I-P-A-W).
- Tagline: **One Platform. Every Animal. One Connected Ecosystem.**
- Brand status: provisional pending formal trademark, company-name, domain, social-handle, GitHub and app-store clearance. Do not claim exclusivity.
- Product is NOT restricted to dogs, cats, household pets, pet owners, or a pet shop. It is an animal-inclusive digital ecosystem.

## Vision and scope
KINNIPAW connects people and organizations involved in animal care, welfare, knowledge, rescue, responsible guardianship and lawful services. The taxonomy must support companion animals, birds, fish, reptiles, small mammals, farm animals, equines, and wildlife as distinct categories where the relevant workflow is lawful and safe. Species/category support must be extensible rather than hard-coded to cats and dogs.

The platform pillars include: animal profiles and care histories; community and education; responsible adoption/rehoming where legally appropriate; lost-and-found and sightings; rescue and volunteer coordination; animal welfare organizations and shelters; veterinary and animal-care professional discovery; reminders and records; moderated messaging; notifications; carefully bounded AI assistance; admin, trust, safety and moderation.

## Safety and legal boundaries
- “Every animal” means inclusive information architecture and welfare support, NOT unrestricted ownership, capture, sale, trade, adoption or transport of every species.
- Never enable wildlife trafficking, protected-species trade, illegal possession, unsafe exotic-animal sales, or unverified animal transfers.
- Adoption/rehoming workflows must be configurable by species, jurisdiction and policy; prohibited species and transactions must be blocked. Farm/livestock workflows, if ever included, require separate legal, welfare and product-policy review.
- Wildlife workflows prioritize rescue, rehabilitation, reporting and qualified organizations; never publicize sensitive nest, den, roost or endangered-animal coordinates.
- Veterinary information is educational only. AI must not diagnose, prescribe, dose medication, or replace qualified veterinary professionals.
- No exact home addresses or live location exposed publicly.

## Geography, language and platforms
- Bangladesh-first launch; design for later regional expansion without assuming all laws are identical.
- Bangla and English from the foundation, including search, validation, notifications, accessibility and Unicode-safe storage.
- Responsive web/PWA plus Android and iOS companion apps.

## Delivery constraints
- Antigravity agents perform implementation; the repository documents are the complete source of truth. No external ChatGPT links or chat retrieval are required or permitted as a project dependency.
- Free-tier-first and local-first. Verify current commercial-use terms, quotas, regions, limits and data policies before selecting a provider. Never incur charges or deploy production without explicit approval.
- Sequential milestone gates: no phase skipping. Each phase requires documented acceptance criteria, tests, review and user approval before proceeding.
- Do not claim “bug-free” or production-ready without measured test evidence, security review, restore test and release checks.

## Reconciliation rule
This file is authoritative over any legacy source wording that narrows the product to pets, dogs, cats or pet owners. Preserve legacy table/API identifiers where changing them would create migration/API churn, but use animal-inclusive domain labels and extensible species taxonomy in new design.
