# Source Archive 01 — Product and Brand Requirements

This file consolidates the approved conversation requirements for the KINNIPAW project.

## Product concept

A Bangladesh-first animal-inclusive community and animal-welfare platform for animal guardians, animal lovers, responsible adopters, rescuers, organizations and veterinary/service providers. The product is intended to be emotionally engaging and genuinely useful, with repeated community use rather than a static directory.

## Required pillars

- Animal profiles and photo timelines.
- Community feed, comments, reactions, follows and bookmarks.
- Responsible adoption listings and applications.
- Lost-and-found reports, sightings and possible matching.
- Rescue requests, assignment and resolution tracking.
- Animal care records and reminders.
- Veterinary/service-provider directory, verification and reviews.
- Community events.
- Messaging and notifications.
- Moderation, report/block and admin tooling.
- AI care education, matching suggestions, listing assistance and moderation assistance, introduced only after core utility works.

## Target platforms

Responsive web, PWA, Android and iOS. Web and mobile must use a shared backend and shared API contracts.

## Market

Initial market: Bangladesh. Bangla and English must be supported. The product should not depend on government integrations or massive external datasets.

## Brand

Working name: KINNIPAW. Tagline: One Platform. Every Animal. One Connected Ecosystem. Provisional pending formal checks.
