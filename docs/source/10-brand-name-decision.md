# KINNIPAW — Brand Naming Decision

KINNIPAW (K-I-N-N-I-P-A-W) is the selected provisional working name. The name was chosen to avoid the narrow “paw”/pet-only implication and support a broad animal-inclusive ecosystem.

Tagline: **One Platform. Every Animal. One Connected Ecosystem.**

This is a working-name decision only. No claim is made that the name, domain, trademark, company name, package namespace, social handles or app-store listing is available. Before public investment, search exact and confusingly similar marks and names in Bangladesh DPDT, WIPO and relevant target territories; company registries; domains; GitHub; social platforms; Google Play and Apple App Store. Obtain qualified legal advice where needed.

Do not use KINNIPAW, Pawzomi, Posumi, Pawmiro or PetBond as the active brand. Historical names may be mentioned only in migration notes when needed to explain legacy material.
